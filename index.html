Setting up lessons:

Hourly Lessons may be scheduled regularly or on a week-to-week basis depending on availability.
Lessons may either be held at my studio located at 1030 Cragmont Ave, Berkeley, or by at-home visits if nearby.
Lessons are at a rate of $60 per session. I strongly believe musical education should be affordable and widely accessible, so if this rate isn't manageable for your student please reach out and I can help with accomodations.


All students start out with a free trial lesson where we can get to know each other, establish musical interests and goals, and see if lessons would be a good fit.
If you are planning on visiting the studio, all I ask is that you call ahead at least a day in advance so that we can ensure a time to visit.
If you would like to visit the studio for your first lesson, I provide access to all of the instruments I teach so the student may explore and discover where their interests lie if just starting out.

Reach out in anyway most convenient!
If leaving a voicemail, be sure to leave the name of the student and perhaps a bit of information regarding which instrument(s) they'd like to learn start learning.
If filling out the form, I will be sure to get back to you as soon as possible.
